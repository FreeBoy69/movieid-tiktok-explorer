# AutoYT Product Context

## Status

This context is inferred from the existing AutoYT workspace and this public landing-page request. It is limited to observable product functionality.

## Product

AutoYT is a creator operations workspace for discovering source channels and videos, scoring candidates, preparing short-form video variations, and scheduling releases to connected channels.

## Primary Audience

Creators and small content teams operating one or more video channels who want repeatable research-to-publishing workflows.

## Public Landing Goal

Explain the real AutoYT workflow clearly and lead visitors to sign in with Google to enter the workspace.

## Product Boundaries

- Do not promise views, subscribers, revenue, or fully autonomous publishing outcomes.
- Do not use third-party creator testimonials, logos, screenshots, brand assets, or claims without authorization.
- Present voice tools as workflows for authorized material only.
